Every water damage restoration job starts with extraction, and no other early step matters more. The faster standing water is removed from your property, the less damage occurs to structural materials, flooring, personal belongings and building systems. Every hour that water sits on surfaces, it penetrates deeper into materials, soaking through carpet into padding, wicking up drywall, saturating wood framing and seeping into subflooring. Across Palm Coast, Palm Coast Restoration handles emergency extraction with industrial portable and truck-mounted machines that can remove hundreds of gallons a minute.

Truck-mounted units rank with the strongest water removal systems made, and their suction lifts water out of hard floors, carpet and structural materials much better than anything you can rent or buy. Confined spaces, wall cavities and the gaps between floor layers call for different gear, so we use portable extractors, specialty tools and submersible pumps in places the truck-mounted hoses cannot get to.

Removing visible water is only part of what professional extraction involves. Using moisture detection equipment, our IICRC-certified team finds water that has moved out of sight, whether under cabinets, beneath flooring, behind baseboards or inside walls. Before drying starts, we use specialty tools to pull as much moisture as we can out of those hidden spots, so structural drying takes far less time and equipment and secondary mold is less likely.

Warning Signs for Water Extraction

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Flooded window wells or stairwells letting water into the building
  • A utility area, mechanical room or crawl space with water in it
  • An appliance, HVAC unit or pipe that is leaking right now
  • Floors flooded by a fire sprinkler or suppression system going off
  • Water seeping out from under baseboards or cabinets
  • Water flowing or dripping from ceilings, walls or fixtures
  • Standing water on any floor surface, whatever the source

Water Extraction Prevention Tips

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Regularly inspect under sinks, around toilets and near appliances for early signs of a leak
  • Leak-sensing shutoff valves can cut off the water on their own when something fails
  • Raise anything you store in the basement a few inches above the floor
  • Know where your main water shutoff valve is and label it clearly
  • Check the supply hoses on your appliances and swap out any that look old or worn
  • Store a bucket and spare towels where you can grab them, along with the number for a restoration company
