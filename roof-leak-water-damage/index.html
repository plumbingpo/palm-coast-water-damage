Roof Leak Water Damage Help in Palm Coast

A roof leak rarely starts where the stain appears. A loose shingle, a failed flashing joint or an aging seal around a skylight lets rain in, and it travels across the roof deck and framing until it falls into the insulation. By the time a brown ring shows on the ceiling, the attic above it has often been wet for a while. We find the full wet area and dry it out.

We are a water damage crew, not a roofing company. Inside the home, our team takes out saturated insulation, dries the wood and drywall, treats any mold and repairs the ceiling. Should the leak still be active, our crew can cover that section with a tarp for now, or time our work with the roofing contractor you pick so nothing gets sealed over an open leak.

Right Now

What to Do Right Now

  1. Shut Off Wet CircuitsWater coming through a ceiling fan, a light or close to an outlet means that circuit should be turned off at the panel, and the fixture should not be touched.
  2. Catch the DripsSet buckets or pans under active drips and move furniture, rugs and electronics out of the way. Lay towels or plastic sheeting down to protect the floor.
  3. Drain a Bulging CeilingWhen water pools in a bubble overhead, pierce the low point with a screwdriver while standing clear, and let it run into a bucket.
  4. Leave the Roof AloneDo not climb onto a wet roof or crawl through a dark attic. From indoors, mark where the water enters, and let professionals handle everything else.
  5. Document What You SeeTake photos and video of the ceiling, the attic and any damaged belongings before you move or throw anything away, then call us to start drying.
Will Insurance Pay?

Does Insurance Cover It?

Interior damage from a sudden, accidental roof leak, like one caused by a fallen limb or wind-torn shingles, is often covered under a homeowners policy. Leaks blamed on age, wear or skipped maintenance are usually denied, and the roof repair itself may be handled differently from the interior. Our team gives your adjuster photos and moisture readings, yet what is covered comes down to your policy, so check it or talk to your agent.

The Usual Culprits

What Causes It

  • Flashing that has rusted, lifted or pulled away around chimneys, vents and walls.
  • Aging, cracked seals around a skylight frame that no longer keep water out.
  • Rubber boots around plumbing vent pipes that split after years in the sun.
  • A valley liner that has worn through at the point where the most runoff passes.
  • Fasteners working loose over time, so water follows the nail shank into the attic.
What Is at Risk

What Happens if You Wait

Drywall Ceilings

Drywall that stays wet stains, sags and loses its strength. The tape releases along cracked seams, and with enough weight a section can come down suddenly.

Mold in the Attic

A dark, warm attic with damp wood and paper-faced drywall suits mold well. Within 24 to 48 hours it can start growing, then move across the bottom of the roof deck.

Attic Insulation

For days after a leak, matted insulation sits wet on top of the ceiling and no longer insulates. Fiberglass can sometimes be dried. Soaked cellulose usually has to be removed.

Ceiling Fixtures and Wiring

Recessed cans, ceiling fans and electrical boxes are low points where roof water collects and drips through. Moisture in a fixture can cause a short and later corrosion, which is why it needs inspection before use.

Our Process

How We Handle Roof Leak Water Damage

1

Map Where Water Went

We inspect the attic, ceilings and wall cavities with moisture meters and thermal imaging to map everywhere the rain traveled, including areas that still look dry from the room below.

2

Cover and Protect

When rain can still get in, a temporary tarp from us or a call to your roofer comes first. Anything sitting beneath the damage, including the flooring, gets protected too.

3

Remove and Dry

Unsalvageable drywall and insulation come out, and dehumidifiers and air movers go into the attic space and each room under it, with readings taken until the framing has dried.

4

Treat and Rebuild

Where it is called for, we apply an antimicrobial to the bare framing. After the roofer has finished, new insulation goes in, fresh drywall is hung and finished, and the ceiling is primed and painted to match.
